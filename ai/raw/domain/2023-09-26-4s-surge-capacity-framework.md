## Notes

- The article credits the 4-S framework of surge capacity to Barbisch and Koenig.
- The framework has 4 domains:
  - staff: appropriately trained personnel.
  - stuff: durable and perishable supplies and equipment.
  - structures: physical facilities and structures where healthcare happens.
  - systems: management policies and procedures that integrate operations inside a healthcare organization, or between it and external supporting entities.
- A "popularly modified version" of the framework replaces structures with the term space, to stress the physical nature of the facilities.
- The study coded each surge practice with one primary domain. Practices with overlapping functions also got a secondary domain.
- The study identified 32 notable practices at 5 pilot sites. Systems was the most prevalent domain. Systems and staff were the 2 domains that co-occurred most often.
- ASPR TRACIE summary of the Kaji, Koenig and Bey paper: the authors highlight the difference between daily and disaster surge, and give an overview of the essential components of surge capacity.
